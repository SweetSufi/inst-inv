# THE INSTITUTE OF UNNECESSARY RESEARCH
## Investigation 11 — Rabbit Hole
### One-Shot Build Prompt for Claude

Build a complete, functional, responsive, independently deployable microsite titled **Investigation 11 — Rabbit Hole** for the Institute of Unnecessary Research.

**The central premise:** Every answer opens a deeper question.

This investigation should feel like an intellectual adventure through an extraordinary Victorian research archive, guided by Alice, a contemporary young explorer.

The experience should be imaginative, atmospheric, surprising, and genuinely informative.

Give the visitor the sensation of descending into increasingly specialized knowledge, discovering connections they would not ordinarily have considered.

## 1. Primary Applied AI Capability

**Progressive Knowledge Exploration**

Organizes related concepts into increasingly detailed pathways for research discovery and navigation.

The experience must demonstrate this capability through meaningful, connected exploration rather than simply presenting a collection of unrelated facts.

Visitors should understand how each discovery relates to the previous one.

## 2. Primary Prompt Engineering Method

**Prompt Chaining**

Plain-language definition:

Prompt chaining breaks a complex task into connected steps, where the output of one step helps guide the next.

For this investigation, use prompt chaining as the primary methodology for developing the research content and structuring the exploration pathways.

The intended sequence is:

1. Introduce and explain a subject.
2. Identify meaningful connections arising from that explanation.
3. Develop more specialized information about the selected connection.
4. Continue the investigation while preserving the context of previous discoveries.
5. Present a coherent record of the visitor's research journey.

**Important technical distinction:** This initial version is a curated, self-contained application. It must not depend on a live AI API.

Use a predeveloped knowledge structure and deterministic branching during visitor interactions.

Do not describe the deployed application as performing live prompt chaining unless actual model inference and contextual prompt passing are implemented.

## 3. Approved Visual Guidepost

Use the attached approved illustration as the visual reference.

The environment is an immense Victorian-inspired research archive with circular galleries, descending staircases, architectural arches, warm amber lighting, and increasingly mysterious blue illumination at lower levels.

The atmosphere should be scholarly, cinematic, imaginative, and quietly extraordinary.

Maintain the restrained visual density of the approved image. Avoid excessive decoration or clutter.

**Alice**

Alice is a young, brown-skinned explorer with long dreadlocks, oversized electric-blue glasses, and a futuristic navy exploration jumpsuit.

Her visual identity includes a vivid blue hair bow, subtle technical detailing, a compact backpack, and metallic high-top sneakers with bright pink accents.

Maintain the contemporary illustrated character style established by the supplied reference.

Alice should appear naturally integrated into the environment and serve as a recognizable visual guide.

Preserve the juxtaposition between her futuristic appearance and the historical architecture.

The archive includes dignified, brown-skinned sculptural busts representing intellectual and cultural achievement.

Do not include references to Alice in Wonderland, playing cards, teapots, rabbits, or recognizable characters or objects from that story.

The illustration is an art-direction reference, not a static website mockup. Build functioning interface elements independently.

## 4. Visitor Experience

Begin with five curated starting questions:

1. Why do we dream?
2. How did people decide what time it is?
3. Why do some buildings make us uncomfortable?
4. How did ordinary objects become sacred?
5. Why do humans collect things?

Each question should open a research pathway containing approximately four levels of exploration.

At each level:

- Present a concise, engaging, factually grounded explanation.
- Offer two or three meaningful directions for deeper exploration.
- Allow the visitor to choose a direction.
- Preserve the relationship between the original question and subsequent discoveries.
- Make each successive level feel more specialized and intellectually revealing.

Avoid superficial trivia, arbitrary tangents, or fabricated connections.

The experience should be substantial enough to reward curiosity while remaining accessible to non-specialists.

Research content should be prepared in advance, using credible sources. Include source references that support substantive factual claims. Do not invent citations.

## 5. The Descent

The architecture should communicate increasing depth.

Use transitions, movement, lighting, or spatial changes to create the sensation of traveling farther into the archive.

The visitor should experience one coherent environment rather than a disconnected collection of screens.

Allow the approved visual guidepost to inform the overall experience while retaining creative freedom in the interface design.

Motion should be subtle, purposeful, and responsive to reduced-motion preferences.

## 6. The Research Trail

Provide a persistent, accessible way for visitors to inspect the sequence of discoveries they have made.

The Research Trail should allow visitors to:

- See their original question.
- Review the discoveries and connections followed.
- Return to an earlier point.
- Explore an alternative branch.
- View a final summary of their completed journey.

Where practical, provide a way to copy or save the completed trail.

This feature should demonstrate that knowledge exploration is cumulative and contextual.

## 7. Technical Requirements

Build a complete functional microsite using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

The initial implementation must be:

- Self-contained and deployable on Netlify.
- Functional without API keys or external AI services.
- Responsive across desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- Accessible through keyboard navigation.
- Capable of maintaining exploration state.
- Able to support meaningful branching without broken pathways.

Organize the knowledge content and navigation logic so that future integration with a live AI service is possible without redesigning the entire interface.

Do not implement or imply live AI functionality in this release.

All visible navigation and interaction controls must work.

- **Nunito Sans** for body text, explanations, controls, and functional interface content.

Use the supplied licensed font assets where available and preserve the Institute's established typographic hierarchy.

Do not substitute fonts silently.
