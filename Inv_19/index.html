Behind the Investigation: Research Note

Behind the Investigation explains the generative AI capabilities, technologies, and design decisions demonstrated by each Institute investigation.

Institution
The Institute of Unnecessary Research
Investigation
19 — Museum After Dark
Primary capability
Immersive environmental storytelling & interactive world simulation
Research question
How can a single illustrated environment, simple interactions, sound, and restrained animation create the impression of a living museum?
Method
AI-assisted visual development and website construction using an approved watercolor panorama and a revised implementation prompt.
How it works
Visitors explore a fixed illustrated panorama and select the women, the policeman or the market painting to activate contextual sound recordings. Selecting the market painting also brings small, restrained movements to the painted market scene.
Technical implementation
Browser-based interaction logic, image-relative hotspots, supplied MP3 recordings mixed with the Web Audio API, and restrained visual effects, including aligned overlay layers cut from the approved painting for the market animation. No live LLM inference: nothing is generated while a visitor uses the site.
What this demonstrates
An immersive audiovisual museum: AI-assisted visual composition combined with contextual sound design, restrained visual effects, accessible navigation, and environmental atmosphere.
Limitations
Characters remain embedded in the panorama and are not autonomous or independently animated; only the market painting shows small local movements, built from the flattened image. Atmospheric recordings are not historical documentation.
