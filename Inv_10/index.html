THE INSTITUTE OF UNNECESSARY RESEARCH / INVESTIGATION 10

Behind the Investigation: Research Note

Behind the Investigation explains the generative AI capabilities, technologies, and design decisions demonstrated by each Institute investigation.

Primary Capability

Multilingual generation and cross-cultural interpretation.

Research Question

How can an interactive language system show the cultural and emotional differences between related expressions without treating them as exact equivalents?

Method

One-shot AI-assisted development guided by an approved visual reference and an interactive language experiment.

How It Works

Visitors select curated expressions and compare their cultural context. A kinetic typographic mobile animates the change between expressions. The Bureau maps a visitor-selected feeling or situation to a curated expression using a fixed lookup.

Technical Implementation

Rule-based, not a live LLM. The collection and comparisons are editorially authored; the Bureau uses a predefined selection-to-expression lookup. AI assisted in creating the website, but no model generates answers during a visitor session.

Limitations

The small collection cannot capture every nuance, dialect, or context. Bureau suggestions are approximations, not authoritative translations. Multilingual typography depends on available browser glyph support.

What This Demonstrates

Interaction design for multilingual interpretation, structured content, accessible motion, and transparent AI-assisted implementation.
